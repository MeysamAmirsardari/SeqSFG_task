A 'no' trial here has no element structure at all: the same 30 channels, the same per-channel
budget, the same 1560 tones, every one placed at a free time at random. Nothing starts together
anywhere. It is the only absent class where "was a figure there?" means what it says.

WHY THE DENSITY IS 52. Synchrony is an envelope event, and no arrangement of the same tones
avoids it, so this contrast cannot be envelope-matched -- only buried. Learnt observer over all
65 features, fitted within each cell, 40+40 a cell:

64 is the ceiling: above it per-channel occupancy passes 0.6 and the validator refuses, because
the tones can no longer be packed without overlap. 52 takes most of the reduction while leaving
the figure among ~15 simultaneous tones rather than ~18. The density is a trade, not a free win:
it masks the figure as well as the cue. Listen at several before committing.

HOW TO READ THE LADDER.

  0 ms   confounded, d' = +3.0, and it cannot be fixed at any density. 0 ms means the components
         are simultaneous, and a simultaneous group is a level event. Report it as a
         manipulation check: it says the listener understood the task, not how far onsets can be
         separated.
  4 ms   confounded, d' = +1.0. Report it, and report that number beside it.
  8 ms and above   clean at every seed. This is the measurement.

The pooled d' from a session will be inflated by the two bottom rungs. Quote the ladder, not the
pool, and put this table next to it.

THE ALTERNATIVE. asynchrony_rising_config.json uses 'roving': an aligned group in both classes,
differing only in whether the same pitches return. That one is clean at every rung, including
0 ms (learnt d' between -0.22 and +0.02 across all six). What it costs is the question: it asks
whether a figure RECURRED, not whether one was there, and its 'no' trials audibly contain
groups. The first listener on it produced hits 0.80 against false alarms 0.75 -- answering "was
a figure there?" truthfully, before the wording was fixed to ask about recurrence.
