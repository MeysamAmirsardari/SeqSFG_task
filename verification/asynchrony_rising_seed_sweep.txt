Read it as a U, not a single leak, and both arms have the same cause: the present element's
onsets sit on an EVEN grid and the absent's are placed at random inside the same window.

  at 0 and 4 ms the grid is a chord, so the present interval has a level event the absent one
  does not. env:n_bursts_3sd is 15.2 in present against 10.0 in absent at 0 ms -- it is not
  subtle, and no arrangement of the same tones avoids it. Synchrony IS an envelope event.

  at 16 and 20 ms the grid is a regular 62 Hz / 50 Hz pulse train inside the element while the
  absent scatter is irregular, so the regularity itself is readable (env:mod_10_30Hz,
  env:kurtosis). Smaller than the chord cue, and marginal at 20 ms, but the sign is positive
  at all five seeds at 16 ms.

  8 and 12 ms are clean: the window is wide enough that a chord is gone and narrow enough that
  seven random placements look much like seven evenly spaced ones.

WHAT TO DO WITH IT. Report the whole ladder, and report this table beside it. d' at 0 and 4 ms
is an upper bound, not a measurement -- treat 0 ms as a manipulation check. 8 and 12 ms are the
clean measurement. 16 ms carries about half a d' of machine-readable cue; 20 ms about a quarter.

The alternative is asynchrony_config.json, whose 'roving' absent is clean at every rung
(pooled p = 0.55, learnt observer d' = -0.03) -- but it asks whether a figure RECURRED rather
than whether anything was bound, which is a different question.

The wide end could be cleaned without changing the question: draw the seven within-element
offsets once per trial from a uniform distribution over the span and hold them fixed across
elements, instead of putting them on an even grid. Sorted and assigned to ascending channels
that is still a rising sweep, and both classes then draw their offsets from the same
distribution, so only recurrence differs. It does not help at 0 ms, where the span is zero and
the two classes collapse onto each other. That construction is not implemented.
