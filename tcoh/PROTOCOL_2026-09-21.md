# Two-tone coherence — repeatability pilot

**Dated 2026-09-21. Written before any new listener was recruited or run.**

Frozen preset: `tcoh/configs/tcoh_curve_v1.json`, config hash **`c72765e1e832a4db`**.
Nothing in this document may be changed once the first new listener has been run. If a defect
forces a change, the preset is re-versioned (`tcoh_curve_v2.json`), this protocol is superseded
by a new dated one, and listeners run under the two versions are reported separately and never
pooled.

---

## 1. What this pilot is, and is not

It is a **feasibility and repeatability pilot**. Six listeners, fixed before recruitment.

It asks four questions:

1. Can listeners do the task at all and yield measurable thresholds?
2. Does the synchrony advantage — better performance at ΔT = 0% than at large lags — recur
   across people?
3. How reproducible is an individual threshold, and an individual curve shape?
4. Is the behaviour near full alternation worth a targeted follow-up?

It is **not** powered to test any mechanism. It contains no scrambled control, no build-up
condition and no B-only ceiling, so it cannot separate temporal coherence from interval
discrimination or from local acoustic overlap, and it cannot compute κ. A monotone rise across
ΔT is predicted by every account on the table and settles nothing on its own. No condition will
be added to this pilot in response to what it finds.

## 2. Participants

**Target: six listeners.** Codes `L01`–`L06`, assigned in order of running.

Eligibility:

- 18–40 years old.
- Self-reported normal hearing, no history of hearing loss, no ear infection or blocked ear on
  the day, no tinnitus that is audible in quiet.
- Not P01. P01's sessions are development data and are stored and reported separately.

Recorded for each listener, in `data_pilot/participants.csv` via the runner's own panel:
age, sex as the participant chooses to report it, handedness, self-reported hearing status,
years of musical training, headphone model, experimenter, consent.

Also recorded in the session notes, by the operator:
- whether the listener reports any hearing difficulty on the day;
- whether they have done a psychoacoustic task before.

No audiogram is collected. That is a limitation and is stated in any write-up: hearing status
is self-reported.

## 3. Apparatus and level

The same machine and the same headphones for all six listeners.

- Output device and system volume are **set once and not touched again** for the whole pilot.
  Both are recorded automatically in every `session.json`, and the runner refuses to continue
  if either has moved since calibration.
- Level is measured with an SPL meter or coupler at the earpiece, before the first listener and
  again after the last.
- Target: **65 dB SPL for one tone**, 68 dB SPL for the two together.
- Tolerance: ±3 dB. Outside that, `tone_amplitude` is corrected with
  `--set tone_amplitude=<value>` (the calibration step prints the exact value) and **not** by
  moving the volume slider, which is an undocumented quantised taper.
- The measured level, the system volume and the device name are recorded in every session.

A session run without a level measurement is marked in the report as nominal-level and is not
compared with any other session on absolute level.

## 4. Instructions and practice

The instruction text is fixed in `tcoh/runner.py` (`INSTRUCTIONS`) and is read verbatim from
the screen. The operator does not paraphrase it and does not mention streams, grouping, the low
tone, or what the experiment is about. A listener told to attend to the relationship between
the two tones is performing a different experiment.

If asked what to listen for, the only permitted answer is the instruction itself: *"in one of
the two sounds the very last high tone is slightly out of place in time."*

Sequence, all automatic:

1. **Familiarisation** — 2 trials at a large shift, answer given.
2. **Practice** — 12 trials at 45 ms, feedback on, criterion 75% correct.
   Up to three rounds. A listener who does not reach 75% in three rounds still proceeds; the
   fact is recorded and reported, and their data is flagged, not discarded.
3. **Main block** — 12 tracks, all six conditions interleaved.

Feedback is on for every trial of the main block. This is 2I-2AFC, which has no criterion for
feedback to distort, and it keeps a listener calibrated over a 34-minute block.

## 5. Breaks, stopping and resuming

- A rest is offered every **80 trials** (roughly every 5–6 minutes) and at the block boundary.
- The listener may rest as long as they like, and may ask to stop at any time by pressing `q`.
- A session may be split across sittings. Resume with `--resume`; the runner rebuilds every
  staircase from the trial log and continues where it stopped. Per-trial randomisation is keyed
  to the slot, so a resumed session is identical to an uninterrupted one.
- Sittings for one listener must be **within 7 days** and use the same calibration.

## 6. Criteria, all fixed in advance

| | rule | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Practice** | 75% correct within three rounds | below: flagged in the report, data kept |
| **Catch** | 45 ms probe at ΔT = 0%, 7% of trials, miss rate ≤ 15% | above: **session not usable** |
| **Convergence** | 6 reversals at the final step size, ≤ 90 trials | a non-converged track has no threshold and is reported as such |
| **Boundary** | a track whose averaged reversals sit on the 50 ms ceiling or the 0.25 ms floor | reported as a **bound**, marked, and excluded from the geometric mean |
| **Timeout** | no response within 6 s | trial logged, not scored, never updates a staircase |
| **Abandonment** | 3 consecutive timeouts | session stops, status `abandoned`, **not usable** |

A condition keeps its geometric mean only if **both** tracks converged and neither is
boundary-limited. A condition with one usable track reports that track alone, marked.

## 7. Unusable and interrupted sessions

Every session that is started is reported, including those that are not usable. The report
lists, for each: the listener code, what happened, and which rule excluded it.

A session is unusable if, and only if:

- catch miss rate exceeds 15%; or
- the session was abandoned on consecutive timeouts; or
- the listener withdrew before completing the main block; or
- a technical fault (audio dropout, wrong configuration) is recorded by the operator at the
  time, not afterwards.

**A curve is never unusable because of its shape.** A listener is not replaced, excluded or
re-run because their thresholds are high, low, flat, non-monotone or unlike anyone else's.

## 8. Replacement and stopping rule

- Run until **six usable** listeners are obtained.
- A listener excluded under §7 is replaced, up to a maximum of **three replacements**. If more
  than three of the first nine listeners are unusable, data collection stops and the failure
  rate itself is the finding — it means the task or the apparatus has a problem that a bigger
  sample will not fix.
- No listener is added beyond six because the result looks unclear.
- No interim analysis decides whether to continue. The group report is produced once, after the
  sixth usable listener.

## 9. Data location

New-listener data goes in **`data_pilot/`**, which contains nothing else. P01's development
sessions stay in `data/` and are never pooled with, or summarised alongside, the six.

## 10. Analysis, specified in advance

`python -m tcoh pilot data_pilot/L*/tcoh_session_* --out pilot_out/`

Produces, per listener: the raw threshold curve in milliseconds, both track estimates at every
lag with their geometric mean, track-to-track repeatability as a log ratio, and the session's
convergence, boundary events, catch score, timeouts, interval-response preference and actual
duration. Then a group overview showing every listener individually alongside a descriptive
group geometric mean.

The ΔT axis runs **100% on the left, 0% on the right**.

Fixed in advance, and not negotiable afterwards:

- The curve is plotted **as measured**. It is not fitted, smoothed or forced monotone.
- The behavioural axis is a **displacement threshold in milliseconds**. It is not labelled
  λ₂/λ₁ and is not rescaled onto it.
- **No κ.** B-only is not measured in this preset, so the denominator does not exist.
- **No bootstrap interval from two tracks.** Repeatability is the log ratio of the two tracks.
  Group spread is computed across **listeners**, with n = the number of people.
- Any model curve is drawn on a **separate axis**, labelled as a visual comparison. The model's
  prediction correlates with a straight line in ΔT at better than r = 0.99 over this range, so
  agreement with it is not evidence for the mechanism.

## 11. What we decide afterwards

| finding | what it supports |
|---|---|
| Curve shape stable across listeners | planning a larger study with controls |
| Synchrony advantage stable, intermediate shape not | a narrower question about synchrony detection specifically |
| Baseline (ΔT = 0%) unreliable across listeners | investigate the task before collecting more data |
| Near-alternation behaviour consistent across listeners | a targeted follow-up on the ΔT = 100% region |

None of these outcomes licenses adding a condition to this pilot.

## Operator checklist

Print this. One tick per line, per session.

**Before the listener arrives**

- [ ] Headphones plugged in; confirm the device name shown by the runner.
- [ ] System volume at the pilot setting; do not adjust it again.
- [ ] `python -m tcoh calibrate --config tcoh/configs/tcoh_curve_v1.json` — measure at the
      earpiece, confirm 65 ± 3 dB SPL for one tone.
- [ ] Quiet room, door closed, phone silenced.

**With the listener**

- [ ] Consent confirmed and recorded in the panel.
- [ ] Eligibility checked (§2); any hearing difficulty today noted.
- [ ] Instructions read from the screen, verbatim. No paraphrase, no mention of streams or
      grouping.
- [ ] Familiarisation and practice completed; practice score written down.
- [ ] Listener knows they may rest at any offered break and may stop with `q`.

**During**

- [ ] Do not sit where the listener can see the screen's feedback line over their shoulder.
- [ ] Do not comment on performance.
- [ ] Note the clock time of any interruption.

**After**

- [ ] Session status printed as `complete`. If not, record what happened.
- [ ] `python -m tcoh analyze data_pilot/<CODE>/tcoh_session_01` — check catch score and
      convergence before the listener leaves, in case a second sitting is needed.
- [ ] Anything unusual written into the session notes **now**, not later.

**After the last listener**

- [ ] Re-measure the level; record it.
- [ ] `python -m tcoh pilot data_pilot/L*/tcoh_session_* --out pilot_out/`
