# Binding bound in a sheared four-tone figure: protocol

**Dated 2026-10-14. Written before any listener was run.**

Frozen preset: `tcbound/configs/tcbound_main.json`, config hash **`5010d4525779b21a`**. Every
session records the config hash, the git commit and whether the working tree was clean. Nothing
in this document changes once the first listener has been run. If a defect forces a change, the
preset is re-versioned (`tcbound_main_v2.json`), this protocol is superseded by a new dated one,
and listeners run under the two versions are reported separately and never pooled.

The design and its rationale are in [README.md](README.md). This document fixes what will be
done and how it will be judged.

---

## 1. Aim

To locate the shear, between 0 and 64 ms of onset asynchrony between adjacent tones, at which a
target tone stops being bound into a repeating four-tone figure. Also, to test whether that
bound is where the temporal-coherence model places it.

## 2. Hypotheses and tests

All tests are across listeners (n = number of usable listeners), one-sided, α = 0.05.

| | hypothesis | measure, per listener | test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 (primary)** | the binding index falls with shear | slope of BI = threshold(B) − threshold(A) over the five steps, against step index 0 to 4 | one-sample t-test, slope < 0; Wilcoxon signed-rank as a robustness check |
| H2 | the timbre weight falls with shear | slope of w over the five steps | as H1 |
| H3 | captors at synchrony lower binding | w(captors) − w(step 0) | one-sample t-test, < 0 |
| H3b | the same, on thresholds | BI(captors) − BI(step 0) | as H3, reported with H3 |

H2 and H3 are corrected together by Holm–Bonferroni. H3b is supporting evidence for H3 and is
not corrected separately.

**H4, manipulation check.** For each listener: is the step-0 threshold for A, and the 64 ms
threshold for B, more than 2 dB below that type's shortcut ceiling (SRS +4.4 dB)? If fewer than
two thirds of listeners pass, the report says the preserved cues were not reliably in use, and
H1 to H3 are interpreted with that limit stated.

**Estimates, reported whatever H1 to H3 show:**

- The bound: s50, the shear at which the group-mean w has fallen half-way from w(0) to w(64).
  Its 95% interval comes from 4000 bootstrap resamples of listeners. If s50 is undefined in more
  than 5% of resamples, no interval is reported and the report says why.
- α under each reading of the model (half-wave, magnitude), from w(s) = a + b·ρ(s/α), with the
  fit's residual sum of squares.
- The captor condition: observed w against the w predicted from the shear conditions' w-to-ρ
  line. Neither the captor condition nor the prediction is fitted.

**Exploratory, labelled as such:**

- B minus B_alone by step: how far the figure still interferes when the target is segregated.
- Per-type decision weights.
- Repeatability across the two sessions: correlation of the BI swing and of the w swing.
- Relation of each listener's bound to years of musical training.

Model predictions, fixed now (`python -m tcbound model`):

| reading | ρ_T at 0, 8, 16, 32, 64 ms | half-way | captors (equivalent shear) |
|---|---|---|---|
| half-wave | 1.00, 0.85, 0.64, 0.37, 0.17 | 18.2 ms | 0.71 (13 ms) |
| magnitude | 1.00, 0.98, 0.94, 0.85, 0.65 | 35.0 ms | 0.81 (37 ms) |

## 3. Design

Within subjects. 13 conditions, each a 3-down 1-up track, all interleaved in one block:

- types A and B at shear steps 0, 8, 16, 32 and 64 ms (10 tracks);
- A and B at step 0 with captors (2 tracks);
- B with the target alone (1 track).

Catch trials make up 6% of trials. Each listener completes **two sessions on different days, 1 to
14 days apart**, with the same preset. A listener's threshold per condition is the mean of the
usable sessions' thresholds. Decision weights pool the trials of usable sessions.

## 4. Participants

**Target: 12 listeners with two usable sessions each.** Codes `L01` onwards, in order of running.

Eligibility:

- 18 to 40 years old.
- Pure-tone thresholds ≤ 20 dB HL at 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 kHz in both ears on the day of session 1.
  The stimulus spans 683 to 3985 Hz. If no audiometer is available, self-report is used instead,
  stated as a limitation, and listeners are not mixed across the two kinds of screening.
- No history of hearing disorder; no tinnitus audible in quiet; no ear infection on the day.
- Not P01, not anyone who has run the timing or level-probe tasks in this repository, and not
  anyone who knows the hypotheses.

Recorded through the runner's panel: age, sex as the participant chooses to report it,
handedness, hearing (audiogram or self-report), years of musical training, headphone model,
experimenter, consent.

**Sample size.** The power simulation runs the real runner and the real analysis on simulated
groups, two sessions per listener (§11). The reference scenario has twelve listeners, each with a
binding swing half what the model predicts: the timbre weight runs from 0.75 to 0.34 instead of
from 1.0 to 0.17. The time scale varies between listeners by a factor of two, and internal noise
by 60%. In that scenario:

- H1, H2 and H3 (on w) were detected in every simulated study.
- H3b (on thresholds) was detected in 85%.
- The bound's interquartile range across studies was 3 ms.

With a quarter of the model's swing, H1 and H2 still held in every study and H3 in 92%. With
eight listeners, H3b fell to 42%. Twelve is therefore the smallest sample that keeps every test
above 80% for an effect half the model's size.

The simulated listener adds the two cues, which is the model the weights assume. Real listeners
will add variance the simulation does not have, so these figures are upper limits. The second
session is for precision and repeatability, not for power.

## 5. Apparatus and level

- One computer, one pair of circumaural headphones, for every listener. A sound booth or a quiet
  room with the door closed.
- Calibration (`python -m tcbound calibrate`) plays the 2241 Hz reference tone at the amplitude of
  one stimulus tone. Measure it with an SPL meter and coupler at the earpiece.
- Target: **55 dB SPL**, tolerance ±2 dB.
- Outside the tolerance, `tone_amplitude` is corrected (the runner prints the value) as long as
  the worst case stays below 0.98 FS. Otherwise the system volume is raised and the level measured
  again.
- The system volume and the output device are recorded in every session. The runner stops if
  either has changed since calibration.
- The level is measured before the first listener, weekly, and after the last listener, and every
  measurement is recorded.

## 6. Procedure

Each session, in order, all automatic once started:

1. **Panel and calibration check.**
2. **Familiarisation** (about 2 min):
   - the target tone alone, twice;
   - the whole figure at step 0 and at 64 ms, twice each;
   - two trials with no rove and the answer given;
   - two roved trials (A at step 0, B at 64 ms) with the answer given.
3. **Practice:** 12 trials drawn from all conditions at SRS +6 dB (ΔL 9.9 dB), feedback on,
   criterion 75%. Up to three rounds. A listener who does not reach criterion still proceeds; it is
   recorded, flagged and reported, and the data are kept.
4. **Main block:** the instructions on screen, then 13 interleaved tracks with feedback on every
   trial. A rest is offered every 60 trials, for as long as the listener likes. The listener may
   stop at any time with `q`.

The instructions are fixed in `tcbound/runner.py` (`INSTRUCTIONS`) and are read from the screen.
The operator does not paraphrase them. The operator does not mention streams, grouping, timbre,
the other tones' levels, or what the experiment is about. If asked what to listen for, the only
permitted answer is the instruction itself: *"in one of the two sounds the target tone is louder
on the last repetition."*

A session interrupted by the listener or by a fault is resumed with `--resume` within the same day
if possible, and at the latest within 7 days. Per-trial randomisation is keyed to the trial's slot,
so a resumed session is identical to an uninterrupted one (`tests/test_tcbound.py`).

## 7. Criteria, all fixed in advance

| | rule | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Catch** | no-rove probes at step 0, ΔL 8 dB, 6% of trials; miss rate ≤ 15% | above: session **not usable** |
| **Practice** | 75% in one of three rounds | below: flagged, data kept |
| **Convergence** | 4 reversals at the 2 dB step within 50 trials | a track that does not converge has no threshold for that session |
| **Ceiling** | a track whose averaged reversals include the cap (SRS +9.5 dB) | threshold enters at the cap and is flagged as a lower bound |
| **Timeout** | no response within 6 s | trial logged, not scored, never updates a track |
| **Abandonment** | 3 consecutive timeouts | session stops, status `abandoned`, **not usable** |

## 8. Unusable sessions, replacement and stopping

A session is unusable if and only if one of these holds:

- its catch miss rate exceeds 15%;
- it was abandoned;
- the listener withdrew before the main block ended;
- the operator recorded a technical fault at the time (audio dropout, wrong preset).

**A session is never unusable because of its results.** No listener is excluded, replaced or
re-run because their thresholds, weights or bound are high, low, flat, non-monotone, or unlike
anyone else's.

- A listener with one usable session is analysed with that session and reported. They do not
  count towards the 12.
- Recruitment continues until 12 listeners have two usable sessions, with at most 6 replacements.
  If more than a third of the listeners run are unusable, data collection stops. That failure rate
  is then the finding: it means a problem with the task or the apparatus that more listeners
  would not fix.
- No interim analysis decides whether to continue. No listener is added after the twelfth
  because the result looks unclear. The group report is produced once.

## 9. Data

New data go in **`data_tcbound/`**, which `.gitignore` excludes along with every other `data_*`
folder. Participant data are never committed. Pilot or development sessions (the experimenters,
P01, the screening preset) go elsewhere and are never pooled with or summarised alongside the
main sample.

## 10. Analysis, as implemented

```bash
python -m tcbound analyze data_tcbound/L01/tcbound_session_01 --out out/   # per session
python -m tcbound group data_tcbound/L*/tcbound_session_* --out group_out/  # the report
```

`tcbound.analysis.group` implements §2 exactly. Fixed in advance:

- Curves are shown as measured: not smoothed, not forced monotone.
- Spread is across listeners. No interval is computed from one or two tracks.
- The behavioural axes are thresholds in dB SRS (and dL) and decision weights. They are not
  relabelled as coherence. The model is drawn on its own axis, labelled as a comparison.
- Sessions recorded under any other config hash are refused, not pooled.

## 11. Power simulation

`tcbound/README.md` describes the simulated listener. In each simulated study, 12 listeners ran
two sessions each through the real runner and analysis. Each listener's binding is
b(s) = 0.5 + 0.5·(ρ(s/α) − 0.5), with α drawn from 0.7 to 1.5 and internal noise from 2.5 to
4.0 dB (timbre) and 1.5 to 3.0 dB (own stream):

| scenario | studies | H1 | H2 | H3 (w) | H3b (BI) | bound, median (IQR) | α, median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 listeners, half the model's swing | 20 | 100% | 100% | 100% | 85% | 19.6 ms (17.9 to 20.9) | 1.00 |
| 12 listeners, a quarter of the swing | 12 | 100% | 100% | 92% | 42% | 20.4 ms (14.7 to 22.8) | 1.07 |
| 8 listeners, half the swing | 12 | 100% | 100% | 100% | 42% | 19.2 ms (15.8 to 20.9) | 0.97 |

The true group bound in these scenarios is about 19 ms: 18.2 ms times the listeners' mean α of
1.05. With 12 to 20 studies per row, each percentage is uncertain by about ±10 points. Reproduce with
`python -m tcbound power --gain 0.5 --listeners 12 --studies 20` (and `--gain 0.25`,
`--listeners 8 --studies 12`); `tcbound/power.py` is the simulation.

## 12. What the outcomes mean

| outcome | reading |
|---|---|
| H1 and H2 hold, s50 near 18 ms, α near 1 under the half-wave reading | binding follows the fast-rate coherence the validated model computes |
| H1 and H2 hold, s50 near or beyond 35 ms | binding integrates over the slow repetition; the magnitude reading, or a longer window |
| H1 and H2 hold, H3 holds, captors land where predicted | the bound reflects coherence, not asynchrony as such |
| H1 and H2 hold, H3 fails | the shear effect is specific to asynchrony; coherence without asynchrony did not unbind |
| H1 and H2 fail, H4 passes | no change in binding within 64 ms of shear: the bound lies beyond this range |
| H4 fails | the preserved cues were not in use, and the task does not measure binding for these listeners |

None of these licenses adding a condition to this study. A follow-up is a new study with a new
protocol.

## Operator checklist

Print this. One tick per line, per session.

**Before the listener arrives**

- [ ] Headphones plugged in; the device name shown by the runner is the usual one.
- [ ] System volume at the study setting; do not touch it again.
- [ ] Level within 55 ± 2 dB SPL this week (calibration log).
- [ ] Quiet room or booth, door closed, phone silenced.

**With the listener (session 1)**

- [ ] Consent recorded in the panel.
- [ ] Audiogram (or self-report) recorded; eligibility checked (§4).

**Every session**

- [ ] `python -m tcbound run --config tcbound/configs/tcbound_main.json --data data_tcbound --code Lxx`
- [ ] Instructions read from the screen, verbatim. Nothing about streams, grouping or timbre.
- [ ] Practice score written down.
- [ ] The listener knows they may rest at any offered break and stop with `q`.
- [ ] Sit where the listener cannot see the screen. Do not comment on performance.
- [ ] Note the clock time of any interruption, and any fault, **at the time**.

**After the session**

- [ ] Status printed as `complete`; if not, record what happened.
- [ ] `python -m tcbound analyze data_tcbound/Lxx/tcbound_session_0N`: check the catch score
      and convergence before the listener leaves.
- [ ] Book session 2 within 1 to 14 days.

**After the last listener**

- [ ] Measure the level again and record it.
- [ ] `python -m tcbound group data_tcbound/L*/tcbound_session_* --out group_out/`
